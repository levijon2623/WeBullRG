SPY · QQQ · IWM · intraday cues · 1 of 2

Three cues, one question each

Two watch for an event and show how often it followed through before. The third speaks up when they disagree.
THRUSTRIKE
Price just broke a whole-dollar strike. Does it reach the next one?
Fires
Price comes from at least 5 bp (0.05%) away and gets 10¢ past the strike, up or down. 9:45–3:30 ET.
Shows
How often past breaks like this reached the next strike before falling 10¢ back through, within 15 minutes.
Reads
10 inputs. Helps: a fast approach, the other indexes breaking too, heavy volume. Hurts: same-day option flow already chasing the break.
NEXTLEG
A lower low, then a lower high. Does the next leg run?
Fires
After a lower low, the bounce turns down while still under the prior high. Flipped for longs. 9:35–3:30 ET.
Shows
How often past setups made 2× the risk before the stop, within 30 minutes. The stop sits just above the bounce.
Reads
9 inputs. Helps: a tight stop, negative dealer gamma, the other indexes moving the same way. Hurts: a big gamma wall at price.
TIEBREAK
The two point opposite ways. Which one still stands?
Fires
THRUSTRIKE and NEXTLEG give opposite directions on one ticker within 2 minutes.
Shows
Both cues’ past follow-through in that situation.
Reads
Which came first, because the earlier one has often failed already. If the break came first, also how far price sits from the strike.
