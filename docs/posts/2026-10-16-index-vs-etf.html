What it adds up to

  1. S&P: the ETF view understates it. SPX added ~$437B of put open interest vs SPY's ~$50B, and Friday's 10/30 SPX delta (−$1.6B) was 3× SPY's. The SPY cards show the small end of an S&P hedge.
  2. Nasdaq: the ETF view is the story. QQQ added ~3× NDX's put notional, and Friday's −$6.2B 10/30 QQQ package had no NDX twin: NDX 10/30 delta was flat and its biggest 10/30 puts traded in single-digit lots.
  3. Russell: index and ETF agree. Similar size, small net delta in both: no one-sided bet.
  4. Filtered out: SPX Dec box spreads (financing at 7000/8000, ~33k a leg). Read leg by leg they look like +$40B of bullish delta; they are market-neutral.
