What it adds up to

  1. Hedging, not shorting. All three bought protection all month and paid for part of it by selling lower puts: the signature of big long books insuring themselves.
  2. 10/30 is the date. On 10/15, SPY's $760 puts (+53k) and QQQ's $715 puts (+51k) were bought the same day, same expiry: the window of FOMC and big-tech earnings.
  3. Friday was a hedge reset. QQQ banked ~$45M on its $715 puts and moved its floor to $700–675, and SPX closed its $7,750/$7,650 puts. Only SPY added new 10/30 protection (+87k $750 / +81k $735 kept).
  4. Calls split three ways. QQQ sold its upside, SPY added a $45M upside bet, IWM kept buying calls and rolling them lower: defensive in tech, two-way in small caps.
